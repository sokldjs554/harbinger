## Selection contract (fixed before new candidate scores)
- Purpose: prioritize 10 assets per customer and prediction date for 30-day breakdown follow-up; probability error is a second guard. This is risk ranking, not a mandate to defer statutory inspections.
- Unit: one asset per common as-of date. Build latest inspection features (maximum age 120 days), refresh historical features at that date, and recompute outcomes in (as_of, as_of+30 days]. No inspection-time labels carried forward.
- Schedule: every 30 days anchored to validation_start. This prevents overlapping outcome windows. Require at least 4 dates spanning at least 90 days and at least 50 distinct assets at EVERY date. These are conservative eligibility floors (5 times the inspection budget, four windows), NOT a power calculation or validated customer thresholds.
- Compare every candidate on identical rows, deterministic asset-ID tie breaking and budget 10 at every date. Equal date weight for precision@10 and Brier. Retain existing minimum class counts and Brier improvement >=0.001.
- Uncertainty: paired cluster bootstrap separately by asset (all dates retained together; resampled assets reranked with deterministic copy IDs) and by date (all assets retained together), 2000 fixed-seed replicates. Require lower percentile bound for precision difference >=0 and Brier improvement >0 in BOTH resampling schemes. One-sided alpha=0.05 divided by number of candidates and 2 metrics. Require positive point precision improvement; no worse individual date precision. These conservative sensitivity checks address repeated assets and calendar effects but do not guarantee coverage under arbitrary dependence or field generalization.
- Candidate chosen among qualifying candidates by highest precision improvement then Brier then name. Insufficient, harmful or unstable candidates retain baseline with all rejection reasons in immutable evidence. Schema 1 Brier-only policies fail closed under revised validation; baseline rollback remains available.
- Future test remains unscored and after selection/activation; an earlier synthetic selection demonstration is historical evidence only.
